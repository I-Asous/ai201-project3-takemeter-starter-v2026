argued: makes a claim and supports it with something specific a reader could check — a link, a quote, a named product, feature, policy or person, a number or date, or a concrete account of how the thing works; vague support ("for years", "everyone knows") does not count
unbacked: states an opinion, judgment or prediction as if it were true, with no specific support; might be right, isn't argued
firsthand: reports what happened when the writer personally used, played, bought or fixed something, either as the whole post or as the support for a claim
quip: a sarcastic line, insult or bare emotional reaction; strip out the tone and no claim is left
